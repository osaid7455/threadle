Threadle — Privacy Policy

Effective 6 September 2026
The short version. Threadle has no accounts and no sign-in. Your puzzles, progress and streak live on your device. The app shows ads between puzzles through Google AdMob, and processes the optional "Remove Ads" purchase through Apple and RevenueCat. Neither we nor our partners receive your name or email from the app.

What stays on your device

Level progress, daily results, streaks, best times and your settings are stored locally on your device. We have no server that receives them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Threadle shows interstitial ads between puzzles and optional rewarded ads for hints. Ads are served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google's SDK may collect device identifiers, coarse location inferred from your IP address, ad interaction data and diagnostic data. If you grant tracking permission when iOS asks, Google may use your advertising identifier to personalise ads; if you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Ad content in Threadle is restricted to a general audience rating.

Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

The one-time "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Apple. RevenueCat, our purchase-verification provider, receives an anonymous app-user identifier and the purchase receipt so the purchase can be restored on your other devices. RevenueCat's policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Sharing your result

The "Share" button opens the iOS share sheet with a short text you can send wherever you choose. Nothing is posted without your action.

Children

Threadle is rated 4+ but is not directed at children under 13, and is not enrolled in the App Store Kids Category. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Decline tracking when iOS asks, or change it later in iOS Settings.
  • Purchase "Remove Ads" to stop interstitial ads entirely.
  • Reset all progress from Settings inside the app, or delete the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
