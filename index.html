Threadle

One thread. Every cell. In order.

Drag one line through the numbers in order and fill every cell of the grid. That's the whole rule, and it gets devious fast.

A new 7×7 puzzle every day, the same for everyone in the world. Keep your streak, share your time, then climb hundreds of levels with walls that get trickier.
