How to play

Start at 1 and drag. Visit the numbers in order — 2, then 3, then 4 — wandering through empty cells on the way. Fill every cell and finish on the last number. Thick lines are walls; the thread cannot cross them.

FAQ

I made a wrong turn.

Drag back along the thread to unpick it, or tap any cell on the thread to cut back to that point. Restart clears the board.

How do hints work?

A hint backs the thread up to your last correct cell and marks the next three cells with dotted rings. One hint a day is free; further hints can be earned by watching a short ad.

Is the daily really the same for everyone?

Yes. Each day's puzzle is generated from the date, so every player worldwide gets the identical grid. Share your time and compare.

I bought Remove Ads on another device.

Open Settings → Ads → Restore purchase. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID.

My streak broke.

The streak counts consecutive days with the daily solved, using your device's local date. Missing a day resets it; a solved day earlier in the streak is never lost from your history.

Can I turn off sound or haptics?

Yes, both toggles are in Settings → Play.
